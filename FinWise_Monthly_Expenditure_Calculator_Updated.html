Build your financial safety net

Insurance is protection against large unexpected costs, not an investment recommendation.

🏥 Health insuranceConsider adequate health cover for yourself and dependants. Check waiting periods, exclusions, co-payments, room limits and hospital network.
🛡️ Term life insuranceIf someone depends on your income, consider pure term cover sized around their needs, liabilities and future goals. Review policy exclusions and claim conditions.
🚗 Asset protectionKeep legally required motor insurance active if you own a vehicle. Consider home/contents cover where it makes sense for your situation.
Before buying any policyCompare the policy wording, premium affordability, coverage, exclusions, claim process and insurer service. Avoid buying solely because of a tax benefit. These are general educational pointers, not personalised financial advice.

Helpful money guidelines

50 / 30 / 20 is a starting pointTry to plan roughly 50% for needs, 30% for wants and 20% for savings/debt goals. Adjust it to your income and responsibilities.
Plan before spendingSet aside money for rent, fees, EMIs and premiums first. Pay at least the full credit-card statement balance by its due date whenever possible.
Prepare for emergenciesBuild a separate, accessible reserve over time. A common goal is 3–6 months of essential expenses; more may be appropriate for variable income.

This standalone demo stores the information you enter in your browser's local storage on this device. It does not send data to a server, but local storage is not encrypted and is not suitable for highly sensitive information. For a real public finance product, add secure server-side authentication, HTTPS, encryption at rest, access controls, backups and a privacy policy.
